## [Master] - 2026/11/03

### Fixed

- MINOR The incident fields of the waveguide ports of the time-harmonic Maxwell solver did not form a wave entering the domain for TE modes when the cross product of the transverse directions defined by the corners pointed outward, and for TM modes when it pointed inward, which resulted in a vanishing excitation. The magnetic field of the TM modes had the wrong sign with the exp(-i omega t) convention and a sign correction was wrongly applied to the magnetic field when the corner frame was flipped. Both are fixed, and a unit test checks that n x H_inc = Y E_inc on the port for TE and TM modes, with both orientations of the corners and complex material properties. [#PR](link)
