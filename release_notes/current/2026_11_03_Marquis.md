## [Master] - 2026/11/03

### Fixed

- MINOR Claude found that the incident fields of the waveguide ports of the time-harmonic Maxwell solver did not form a wave entering the domain for TE modes when the cross product of the transverse directions defined by the corners pointed outward, and for TM modes when it pointed inward, which resulted in a vanishing excitation. Also, the magnetic field of the TM modes had the wrong sign with the exp(-i omega t) convention, and a sign correction was wrongly applied to the magnetic field when the corner frame was flipped. [#PR](link)

- MAJOR The `none` keyword for the normalization left a varying convention between TE and TM mode which was unclear in the documentation. The incident mode is now always normalized so that its transverse electric field on the port is real, with a maximum amplitude of 1, for any mode and medium. With the `power` electromagnetic scaling, this only changes the phase of the solution. With the `none` and `electric_field` scalings, the `electric field amplitude` is now the maximum amplitude of the incident transverse electric field on the port, as documented. A new unit test checks the incident fields, and the analytical solutions of the waveguide port application tests were updated to the new normalization. [#PR](link)
