## [Master] - 2026/11/03

### Fixed

- MINOR Claude found that the incident fields of the waveguide ports of the time-harmonic Maxwell solver did not form a wave entering the domain for TE modes when the cross product of the transverse directions defined by the corners pointed outward, and for TM modes when it pointed inward, which resulted in a vanishing excitation. Also, the magnetic field of the TM modes had the wrong sign with the exp(-i omega t) convention, and a sign correction was wrongly applied to the magnetic field when the corner frame was flipped. [#PR](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/)

- MAJOR The normalization of the incident mode of the waveguide ports was not documented, and the `electric field amplitude` of the `electric_field` electromagnetic scaling was not the amplitude of the incident electric field on the port, contrary to the documentation. With the `none` scaling, the incident mode is the standard solution of a rectangular waveguide, whose longitudinal field (H_z for TE modes and E_z for TM modes) has a unit amplitude, as before. With the other scalings, the incident mode is now divided by the maximum amplitude of its transverse electric field on the port, so the `electric field amplitude` is the maximum amplitude of the incident transverse electric field on the port. This factor is real, so the phase of the incident mode is the same for all the scalings. The results with the `none` and `power` scalings are unchanged, while the amplitude of the fields changes with the `electric_field` and `magnetic_field` scalings. A new unit test checks the incident fields, including for a port that is not aligned with the axes. [#PR](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/)
