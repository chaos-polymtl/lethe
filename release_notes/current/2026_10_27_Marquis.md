## [Master] - 2026/10/27

### Added

- MAJOR This PR adds effective medium electromagnetic properties to the time-harmonic Maxwell solver for unresolved CFD-DEM simulations. The electromagnetic properties of the particles are defined in the new `unresolved dispersed phase 0` subsection of the physical properties (with the new `number of unresolved dispersed phases` parameter), and the fluid-particle mixture is defined by the new `fluid_dispersed_phase` material interaction type (`fluid-dispersed phase interaction` subsection). The `electric permittivity effective medium model` and `magnetic permeability effective medium model` parameters select the `none` or `bruggeman` model. The Bruggeman model computes the effective permittivity (including the conductivity) and the effective permeability of the mixture from the void fraction of the volume-averaged Navier-Stokes solvers. Temperature-dependent properties of the mixture and the `threshold` time coupling strategy are not supported yet with this feature and are left for future PRs.
