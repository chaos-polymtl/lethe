## [Master] - 2026/11/15

### Added

- MINOR This PR adds the 3D flow around a sphere to the automatic validation suite, at Re = 0.1 and Re = 150 with the matrix-based and the matrix-free solvers (drag coefficient). The matrix-free solver of the sphere example uses Q2-Q2 elements with an hp global coarsening multigrid preconditioner. At Re = 0.1, the matrix-free simulation is also carried out on domains enlarged twice and four times, and the drag coefficient extrapolated to an infinite domain is compared with Stokes law. [#2213](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2213)

### Documentation

- MINOR The sphere example now explains the effect of the size of the domain on the drag coefficient at Re = 0.1: on the original domain, the drag coefficient converges to approximately 282 instead of 240 because the domain confines the flow, and the deviation decreases like the inverse of the size of the domain. The size of the domain can be changed with the `domain_scale` variable of `sphere.geo`, and the mesh of the matrix-based simulation at Re = 0.1 is now refined once, since the previous result was close to Stokes law only because the discretization error and the confinement error compensated each other. [#2213](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2213)
