## [Master] - 2026/11/16

### Added

- MAJOR This PR adds Dirichlet, Neumann and Robin boundary conditions for the tracer on the surface of the particles of the sharp-interface immersed boundary method (`lethe-fluid-sharp`), to model surface reactions. They are set in the new subsection `tracer boundary condition` of the subsection `particles` and apply to every particle. The type `dirichlet` imposes the concentration, for example for a reaction limited by the diffusion of the tracer. The type `neumann` imposes the flux of tracer into the particles, and the type `robin` imposes the rate of a surface reaction of order n, alpha C_eff^(n-1) C, plus this flux. As for the velocity, the equation of the degrees of freedom shared by a cut cell and a fluid cell is replaced by a polynomial stencil that extrapolates the solution of a neighbouring fluid cell to the surface of the particle. The flux uses the derivative of this polynomial, given by the new function `IBStencil::derivative_coefficients`. The tracer is no longer solved inside the particles: there, it is a harmonic extension of the solution in the fluid. The stencils and the extension are implemented in the new class `IBScalarConstraints`, which applies to any scalar field discretized with continuous elements.
