## [Master] - 2026/08/23

### Fixed

- MAJOR Fixed a crash (`BDF coefficients were requested without a BDF method`) occurring when a `viscous` or `ramp` initial condition was combined with a transient or `steady_bdf` time-stepping method, as is the case in the flow around a sphere example. These initial conditions temporarily set the assembly method to `steady` to solve a simpler problem, but never restored it. The first time step then requested the BDF coefficients of a steady method. For SDIRK methods, the same issue silently kept the assembly method at `steady` for the whole simulation. The assembly method is now restored at the end of the `viscous` and `ramp` initial conditions of the matrix-based, matrix-free and block Navier-Stokes solvers. [#2101](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2101)
