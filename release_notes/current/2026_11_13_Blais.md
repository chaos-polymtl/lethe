## [Master] - 2026/11/13

### Added

- MAJOR A new `rbvms` option of the `stabilization` parameter adds the residual-based variational multiscale (RBVMS) stabilization of Bazilevs et al. (2007) to `lethe-fluid` and `lethe-fluid-matrix-free`, for constant-density Newtonian single-phase flows. Its stabilization parameters are built from the element metric tensor, so they account for the stretching and shearing of the cells, and its cross-stress and Reynolds-stress terms act as an implicit large-eddy simulation model. [#2211](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2211)

- MAJOR A new `velocity subscale` parameter (`quasi_static` by default, or `dynamic`) of the `stabilization` subsection selects the model of the RBVMS fine-scale velocity. With `dynamic`, the fine-scale velocity is advanced in time with the BDF scheme of the velocity, so a steady state reached by time stepping no longer depends on the time step and the stabilization does not vanish at small time steps. It is available in `lethe-fluid` and `lethe-fluid-matrix-free` for steady and BDF simulations on hypercube meshes; the history of the fine-scale velocity is transferred when the mesh is adapted and saved in the checkpoints, which now include a `<prefix>.fine_scale_velocity` file. [#2211](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2211)
