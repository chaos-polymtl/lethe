## [Master] - 2026/10/18


### Added

- MAJOR The matrix-free VANS operator, used by `lethe-fluid-vans-matrix-free` and `lethe-fluid-particles-matrix-free`, now supports the mass source term, which is the last component of the `source term` of the fluid dynamics. As in the matrix-based solvers, the mass source term is added to the continuity equation and its momentum (mass source times velocity) is added to the momentum equation. The mass source term was previously ignored by the matrix-free solvers. [#2175](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2175)

### Fixed

- MAJOR A time-dependent `source term` of the fluid dynamics was only evaluated when the degrees of freedom were set up (at the start of the simulation and after each mesh adaptation) in `lethe-fluid-matrix-free` and `lethe-fluid-vans-matrix-free`. The time of the source term function was updated at every time step, but the source term precomputed by the matrix-free operator was not. The source term is now recomputed at every time step. Simulations with a source term that does not depend on time are not affected. [#2175](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2175)
- MAJOR The void fraction was never percolated at the end of a time step in `lethe-fluid-vans-matrix-free`, since the solver called `finish_time_step()` instead of `finish_time_step_fd()`. The time derivative of the void fraction was therefore always evaluated with respect to the initial void fraction. Only transient simulations with a void fraction that varies in time (e.g., a time-dependent void fraction function) and `void fraction time derivative = true` are affected. `lethe-fluid-particles-matrix-free` was not affected. [#2175](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2175)
- MINOR The Jacobian of the matrix-free VANS operator read the hessians of the trial functions when `enable hessians in residual` was true, but only evaluated them when `enable hessians in jacobian` was true. The multigrid level operators are always built with the hessians enabled in the residual, so setting `mg enable hessians in jacobian = false` made the level operators read hessians that were never evaluated (an assertion in debug, undefined values in release). The hessians are now read only when they are evaluated. This only affects the preconditioner, not the converged solution. [#2175](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2175)
