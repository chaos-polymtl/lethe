## [Master] - 2026/11/02

### Added

- MAJOR This PR adds the parameters of the quadrature method of moments (QMOM) auxiliary physics, which will model the formation of lipid nanoparticles with the QMOMKit library by transporting regular scalars and the raw moments of the particle size distribution. Its solver is not implemented yet: enabling the physics stops the simulation with an error. It adds the `qmom` parameter in the `multiphysics` subsection, the `qmom degree` parameter in the `FEM` subsection, the `QMOM` subsection (number of scalars, scalar names, number of moments, splitting scheme, nucleation/coalescence/growth switches and the `phase evolution`, `pharma` and `physical constants` subsections), the `boundary conditions qmom` subsection (dirichlet, outlet and none), `QMOM` subsections in the `initial conditions`, `analytical solution` and `source term` subsections and `qmom` subsections in the `linear solver` and `non-linear solver` subsections. The scalars of the initial conditions, boundary conditions, analytical solution and source terms are given as lists of expressions, and their moments as a particle size distribution (normal, lognormal, gamma, Weibull, beta, chi or chi-squared) given by its number density, in particles per m^3, and its parameters, whose raw moments are computed by QMOMKit.
