## [Master] - 2026/11/02

### Added

- MAJOR This PR adds the quadrature method of moments (QMOM) auxiliary physics, which transports the ethanol concentration, the free lipids concentration and the first 2N raw moments of the particle size distribution (N quadrature nodes), and which relies on the optional QMOMKit library for the phase-space evolution. All the fields share one discontinuous Galerkin space and are advected by the fluid velocity with an upwind flux and an explicit SSP-RK scheme (ssprk1, ssprk2 or ssprk3), sub-cycled within each fluid time step to satisfy a CFL bound. The transport and the phase-space step are combined with Lie or Strang splitting. The phase-space step calls `apply_qmom_sources`, whose nucleation, growth and coalescence sources are not implemented yet. It adds the `qmom` parameter in the `multiphysics` subsection, the `qmom degree` parameter in the `FEM` subsection, the `QMOM` subsection (number of nodes, splitting scheme, time integration, phase-space evolution, nucleation/coalescence/growth switches, operating conditions and physical constants), the `boundary conditions qmom` subsection (dirichlet, outlet and none), `QMOM` subsections in the fluid `physical properties`, in the `initial conditions` and in the `analytical solution`, and a `qmom` subsection in the `linear solver` subsection. Enabling the physics in a build without QMOMKit throws an error. [#XXXX](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/XXXX)
