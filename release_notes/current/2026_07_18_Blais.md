## [Master] - 2026/07/18

### Added

- MAJOR A new `rbvms` option of the `stabilization` parameter implements the residual-based variational multiscale (RBVMS) stabilization of Bazilevs et al. (2007) for constant-density Newtonian flows, in both `lethe-fluid-matrix-free` and `lethe-fluid`. Its stabilization parameters are built from the element metric tensor, which makes them sensitive to the stretching and shearing of the cells, and it adds the cross-stress and Reynolds-stress terms of the fine-scale velocity, which act as an implicit large-eddy simulation model. The `pspg_supg`, `gls` and `grad_div` stabilizations are unchanged. [#2063](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2063)

- MAJOR A new `velocity subscale` parameter (`quasi_static` or `dynamic`) of the `stabilization` subsection selects the model of the RBVMS fine-scale velocity. With `dynamic`, the fine-scale velocity is advanced in time with the BDF scheme of the velocity, so that a steady state reached by time stepping no longer depends on the time step and the stabilization does not vanish at small time steps. It is available with `lethe-fluid-matrix-free` for steady and BDF simulations, without mesh adaptation in transient simulations or the mortar method, and the history of the fine-scale velocity is saved in the checkpoint files for restarts. [#2063](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2063)
