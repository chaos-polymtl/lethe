## [Master] - 2026/07/18

### Added

- MAJOR A new `rbvms` option was added to the `stabilization` parameter of the Navier-Stokes equations, implementing the residual-based variational multiscale (RBVMS) formulation of Bazilevs, Calo, Cottrell, Hughes, Reali & Scovazzi, "Variational multiscale residual-based turbulence modeling for large eddy simulation of incompressible flows", CMAME 197 (2007) 173-201. It is available in both the matrix-free and the matrix-based incompressible solvers for Newtonian fluids (the matrix-based path uses a new `RBVMSNavierStokesAssemblerCore` assembler, selected like the existing `pspg_supg` and `gls` cores). RBVMS builds the stabilization parameters `tau_M` (eq. 64) and `tau_C` (eq. 65) from the element metric tensor G (eq. 66) and metric vector g (eq. 69) obtained from the mapping inverse Jacobian, making them anisotropy-aware, and adds the fine-scale cross-stress and Reynolds-stress terms (eq. 72). Since Bazilevs et al. define the reference coordinates on the bi-unit cell `[-1,1]^d` whereas deal.II uses `[0,1]^d`, and since the element size used by the other stabilizations is `h/k`, the inverse Jacobian is scaled by `2k` (`k` being the degree of the velocity interpolation) before G and g are assembled. With this scaling, `tau_M` and `tau_C` recover the `h/(2|u|)` and `|u|h/2` limits of the `pspg_supg` and `gls` stabilizations for advection-dominated flows, and the inverse-estimate constant is the degree-independent `C_I = 9`. The existing `pspg_supg`, `gls` and `grad_div` models are unchanged. RBVMS is rejected with an explicit error for non-Newtonian fluids. [#2063](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2063)

- MAJOR A new `velocity subscale` parameter (`quasi_static|dynamic`, default `quasi_static`) was added to the `stabilization` subsection to select the model of the RBVMS fine-scale velocity u'. All the RBVMS terms are now written in terms of u'. With `quasi_static`, u' = -tau_M r_M as before, and `tau_M` contains the `4/dt^2` term, so a steady state reached by time stepping depends on the time step and the pressure stabilization vanishes at small time steps. With `dynamic`, u' is advanced in time with its own equation, d(u')/dt + u'/tau_s = -r_M, discretized with the BDF scheme of the velocity: u' = -tau_t (r_M + m'), where `tau_t = (beta_0 + 1/tau_s)^(-1)`, `tau_s` is `tau_M` without the time step, and the memory m' gathers the fine-scale velocities of the previous time steps, which are stored at the quadrature points. `tau_C` is built from `tau_s`. A steady state reached by time stepping is then independent of the time step. The `dynamic` option is available with the matrix-free solver (`lethe-fluid-matrix-free`) for steady simulations and BDF time-stepping methods, without mesh adaptation in transient simulations, restarts or the mortar method; it is rejected with an explicit error otherwise. [#2063](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2063)
