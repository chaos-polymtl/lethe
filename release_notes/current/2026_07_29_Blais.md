## [Master] - 2026/07/29

### Fixed

- MINOR The time-averaged Reynolds shear stresses (`reynolds_shear_stress_uv`, and in 3D `reynolds_shear_stress_vw` and `reynolds_shear_stress_uw`) were computed by `AverageVelocities` but were no longer written to the output files, so only the normal stresses and the turbulent kinetic energy appeared in the results. The `add_data_vector` call for the shear stresses was dropped when the output was refactored into `OutputStructSolution` objects, leaving the corresponding name and component interpretation vectors unused. The shear stress output struct is now added back in `NavierStokesBase::write_output_results`. [#2074](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2074)
