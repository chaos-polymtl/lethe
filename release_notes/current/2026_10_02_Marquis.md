## [Master] - 2026/10/02

### Changed

- MINOR This PR makes the local assembly of the time-harmonic Maxwell solver faster and reduces its memory consumption. The real and imaginary parts of the electric and magnetic fields share the same real base functions, so the local DPG system is now assembled from real dot products of the base functions instead of complex tensor products of all the shape functions. The symmetry of the Gram matrix is used, the skeleton terms only involve the base functions associated with each face, and the static condensation uses Cholesky solves instead of explicit inverses. In the tested cases (waveguide and fichera-oven examples, temperature coupling test), the assembly is 12 to 17 times faster and the solver 3 to 15 times faster. The results only change at the round-off level. [#PR](link)
