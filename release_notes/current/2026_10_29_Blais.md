## [Master] - 2026/10/29

### Added

- MAJOR `lethe-parameter-check` has a new `--schema solver_family dimension` option, which prints all the parameters of a solver family in the JSON format, with their default value, documentation and pattern, in the order of their declaration. It is meant for tools, such as editors or graphical interfaces, that need to know the parameters of Lethe. The existing usage of `lethe-parameter-check` is unchanged. [#PR](link)
