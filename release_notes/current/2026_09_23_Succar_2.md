## [Master] - 2026/09/23

### Fixed

- MINOR This PR fixes two things in solve_linear_system_and_update_solution: the solver now takes into account a relative tolerance and multiplies it by the L2-norm of the rhs of the linear system to get the solver's tolerance. It is also possible to scale the residual by dividing it by the square root of the triangulation volume, when rescale residual=true. The tolerance printed corresponds to the scaled residual multiplied by the relative tolerance passed in the prm, while the minimum tolerance limits this value. In reality, the linear solver uses the unscaled residual and multiplies the tolerance obtained in the previous step by the square root of the volume of the triangulation, when rescale residual=true. Nonetheless, this feature is not used currently for the void fraction linear solver. [#2132](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2132)
