## [Master] - 2026/11/23

### Added

- MINOR This PR adds the sedimentation of two spheres (drafting, kissing and tumbling, section 4.3 of Barbeau et al., Computers & Fluids 268 (2024) 106094) to the automatic validation suite. The case is simulated with Q1Q1 elements on a mesh of h = 1/128 cm, and the velocities, the kissing, overtaking and bottom times and the RMS errors during drafting are compared with the paper, with Nie & Lin (2020) and with Apte et al. (2009). The Q2Q1 parameter file is also provided, but it is not run in the validation because of its cost. [#XXXX](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/XXXX)
