## [Master] - 2026/11/22

### Changed

- MINOR `Shape::distance_to_shape` and `Shape::distance_to_shape_with_cell_guess` are renamed `half_distance_to_shape` and `half_distance_to_shape_with_cell_guess`, since they return half the distance between the two shapes. [#2230](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2230)

### Fixed

- MAJOR The particle-particle contact of the sharp immersed-boundary solver used half the overlap of the two particles in the Hertz model, which expects the full overlap, as the particle-wall contact already did. The bug was introduced by #979 (2024-02-07) and affects the releases v1.0 to v1.2.0. For a given overlap, the elastic part of the normal force was 0.35 of the Hertz force, the normal damping 0.84 of its value and the tangential stiffness 0.71 of its value. Collisions were too soft and, with a restitution coefficient below 1, too dissipative: a coefficient of 0.5 gave a rebound of 0.37. This changes the results of all the cases with particle-particle contacts. [#2230](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2230)
