## [Master] - 2026/10/21

### Fixed

- MAJOR The DEM sub-stepping of the sharp immersed-boundary solver evaluated the fourth stage of its RK4 scheme at the middle of the sub-step instead of its end, and the orientation was advanced with the angular velocity of the next stage. The scheme is now exact for a constant acceleration. This changes the results of all the cases with contacts or lubrication. [#XXXX](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/XXXX)
- MAJOR In the sharp immersed-boundary solver, a particle loaded from a file with `integrate_motion = false` advanced by one time step at each Newton iteration instead of each time step, its orientation did not accumulate, and the DEM sub-stepping moved it by one CFD time step at each RK stage. The particle now moves with the constant velocity and angular velocity of the file. [#XXXX](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/XXXX)
- MAJOR The point of each boundary used to represent the wall in the particle-wall contact of the sharp immersed-boundary solver was not the closest one to the particle, neither within a process nor when merging the processes. This affected curved walls and boundary ids that cover several planes. [#XXXX](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/XXXX)
- MAJOR The effective shear modulus of the contact model of the sharp immersed-boundary solver had two typos, which changed the tangential force when the two bodies have different Young's moduli or Poisson ratios. It now matches the one of the DEM. [#XXXX](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/XXXX)
- MAJOR The lubrication force of the sharp immersed-boundary solver on a particle was the force of the last particle or wall processed, and a far one reset it to zero. It is now the sum of the forces of all the particles and walls within the lubrication range. [#XXXX](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/XXXX)

### Removed

- MAJOR The particle-wall collision statistics of the DEM post-processing are no longer available in `lethe-fluid-sharp`, since the wall representation of the sharp solver does not allow detecting the start and the end of the collisions reliably. Enabling them now throws an error at startup, as for the DEM lagrangian post-processing. [#XXXX](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/XXXX)
