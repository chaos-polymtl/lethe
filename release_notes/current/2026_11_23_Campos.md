## [Master] - 2026/11/23

### Added

- MAJOR This PR adds the transport of the scalars of the quadrature method of moments (QMOM) auxiliary physics, which no longer requires `number of scalars` to be 0. In the `lie` splitting, the scalars are transported in physical space after the moments, with their source terms, and before the evolution of the moments in phase space, which uses their values at the end of the transport. [#PR](link)
- MAJOR This PR adds the nucleation, growth and coalescence models of the `lipid_nanoparticle_production` process of the QMOM physics, following Shin et al. (2025): classical nucleation with nuclei at the critical size, growth with Ostwald ripening, and Brownian coalescence with the Fuchs attachment efficiency limited by the PEG lipids. They replace the `growth`, `nucleation` and `coalescence` subsections for this process, and compute the supersaturation of the lipid from the local ethanol and free lipid concentrations, in kg/m^3. At the end of the phase-space step, the free lipid concentration loses the mass of lipid gained by the particles. The `lipid nanoparticle production` subsection has new parameters for the lipid properties and the kinetics (`lipid molecular volume`, `lipid molar mass`, `lipid density`, `nucleation factor`, `surface tension`, `nuclei standard deviation`, `growth factor`, `peg factor`, `surface potential` and `hamaker constant`). [#PR](link)

### Removed

- MAJOR This PR removes the `flow rate ratio`, `total flow rate`, `mixing time scale` and `initial concentration organic` parameters of the `lipid nanoparticle production` subsection of the QMOM physics, which were not used: the composition of the mixture is given by the transported ethanol concentration, and the initial and inlet concentrations of ethanol and free lipids are given by the scalars of the initial conditions and of the Dirichlet boundary conditions of the QMOM fields. [#PR](link)
