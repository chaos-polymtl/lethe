## [Master] - 2026/10/08

### Fixed

- MAJOR The random offsets of the volume insertion were regenerated from the same seed at every insertion step, so every batch of particles received exactly the same offsets. In 3D the offsets along the first and third insertion directions were also identical. The offsets are now drawn independently for each direction from a pseudo-random number engine seeded with `insertion prn seed`, the particle type being inserted and the number of particles of that type remaining. Each batch therefore receives new offsets, and a restarted simulation inserts the same particles as an uninterrupted one. In the ray-tracing solver, the two angular offsets of each photon were generated from the same seed and were therefore correlated; they are now independent. [#2153](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2153)
