## [Master] - 2026/10/08

### Fixed

- MAJOR The random offsets of the volume insertion were regenerated from the same seed at every insertion step, so every batch of particles received exactly the same offsets. In 3D the offsets along the first and third insertion directions were also identical. A pseudo-random number engine seeded from `insertion prn seed` and the insertion step count now generates independent offsets for each direction and each batch, and the insertion step count is stored in the checkpoint. Checkpoints written before this change can still be restarted. In the ray-tracing solver, the two angular offsets of each photon were generated from the same seed and were therefore correlated; they are now independent. [#PR](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/PR)
