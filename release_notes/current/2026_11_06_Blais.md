## [Master] - 2026/11/06

### Fixed

- MAJOR The time-averaged Reynolds stresses and turbulent kinetic energy (`calculate average velocities`) were wrong at every degree of freedom that is not located at a cell vertex when a mesh object carries more than one degree of freedom per velocity component. This affected velocity degrees of 3 and more (e.g., Q3-Q3), unequal velocity and pressure degrees (e.g., Q2-Q1), bubble-enriched elements, and simplex elements of degree 3 and more, in all the fluid dynamics solvers. The Reynolds stresses were computed from groups of consecutive degrees of freedom assumed to be the velocity components of a single point, but such groups mixed degrees of freedom of different components and different support points. In `lethe-fluid-block`, the Reynolds stresses were also wrong for Q1-Q1 and Q2-Q2 elements, since the solver passed the wrong group size. The velocity components of every support point are now paired through the finite element, and the turbulent kinetic energy is stored at the pressure degree of freedom located at the same support point. The time-averaged velocities and pressure were not affected, nor were the Reynolds stresses with Q1-Q1 and Q2-Q2 elements in `lethe-fluid`, `lethe-fluid-matrix-free` and the solvers derived from them. The checkpoints of the affected simulations store wrong accumulated Reynolds stresses: the time-averaging must be started over after a restart from such a checkpoint, for example by setting `initial time for average velocity` after the restart time.
