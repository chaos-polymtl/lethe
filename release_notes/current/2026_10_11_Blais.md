## [Master] - 2026/10/11

### Added

- MAJOR The `scalar limiter lower bound` and `scalar limiter upper bound` parameters were added to the `stabilization` subsection. They prescribe bounds within which the solution of the discontinuous Galerkin (DG) formulation of the tracer physics is kept when a scalar limiter (`moe` or `kuzmin`) is used. After the limiter is applied, the deviation of the solution from its cell average is rescaled, in every cell in which the solution exceeds a bound, by the largest factor between zero and one for which the solution remains within the bounds, following the scaling limiter of Zhang and Shu (2010). Contrary to the bounds of the `moe` and `kuzmin` limiters, which are established from the neighboring cells, these bounds also apply at the vertices located on the boundaries of the domain, which the `kuzmin` limiter does not use and at which the solution was consequently not bounded. The rescaling conserves the tracer since it preserves the cell averages. For the same reason, the solution in a cell of which the average is outside of the bounds is set to its average. By default, no bound is prescribed and the results do not change. [#XXXX](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/XXXX)
