## [Master] - 2026/10/29

### Added

- MAJOR The particle temperature of thermal CFD-DEM simulations (`solver type = dem_mp`) can now be projected onto the fluid mesh with the QCM, using the new parameter `project particle temperature` of the `void fraction` subsection. The projected field is registered in the multiphysics interface under the new `PhysicsID::particle_temperature`, so the other physics can read its DoFHandler, mapping and solution through the generic getters of the interface. For this purpose, the DoFHandler of the QCM fields of the particle projector is now stored as a shared pointer. [#2191](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2191)

### Fixed

- MAJOR The QCM projection of the particle fields (fluid forces, drag, particle velocity, momentum transfer coefficient and, now, particle temperature) did not distribute the hanging-node constraints after solving its linear system. On meshes with hanging nodes, the projected fields were therefore zero at the hanging nodes. The constraints are now distributed after the solve. [#2191](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2191)
