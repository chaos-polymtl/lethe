## [Master] - 2026/10/27

### Changed

- MINOR clang-tidy now runs the `bugprone-integer-division` and `bugprone-throw-keyword-missing` checks. To satisfy the second one, `ShapeGenerator::initialize_shape_from_vector` now throws `ExcNotImplemented` for a shape type it cannot build, instead of constructing the exception without throwing it and returning a null shape. The base `Shape::displaced_volume` keeps returning 0 for shapes whose volume is not computed (superquadric and composite), which callers interpret as unknown and replace by the volume of the equivalent sphere. [#PR](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/PR)

### Fixed

- MAJOR The lubrication force of the sharp-interface immersed boundary solver, between two particles and between a particle and a wall, used the integer division `3 / 2`, which evaluates to 1, as its 3/2 prefactor. The force was therefore 2/3 of the documented model. The prefactor is now 3/2, which changes the results of any simulation in which coupled particles come within the `lubrication range max` of each other or of a wall. [#PR](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/PR)
