## [Master] - 2026/10/14

### Added

- MINOR The new `contrib/validation/compare_validation_runs.py` script, called at the end of `validate_lethe.sh`, compares the data files of every validation case with those of the two previous runs. It flags the cases whose results changed, which produced no data or plot, or whose wall time changed significantly. It writes a report in the run folder and posts a summary to Slack when `LETHE_VALIDATION_SLACK_WEBHOOK` is set. The rotating drum validation now also stores its velocity profiles, and every validation case removes the outputs of a previous post-processing before running it, so that a failed post-processing cannot go unnoticed.
