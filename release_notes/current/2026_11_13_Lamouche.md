## [Master] - 2026/11/13

### Changed

- MAJOR The fluid dynamics and CFD-DEM solvers now write their checkpoints in two alternating slots (`<filename>_0.*` and `<filename>_1.*`), like DEM, with the slot of the last checkpoint in `<filename>.checkpoint_controller`. A simulation interrupted while writing a checkpoint can therefore restart from the previous one. DEM simulations now also write a checkpoint at their last time step. Fluid dynamics and CFD-DEM checkpoints written by earlier versions of Lethe cannot be used. [#2209](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2209)
