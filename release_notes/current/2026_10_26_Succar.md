## [Master] - 2026/10/26

### Fixed

- MINOR In the Newton and inexact Newton non-linear solvers, when the line search falls back to the previous step length because a smaller alpha increased the residual, the solution was reset to the previous alpha but the right-hand side was not reassembled. The convergence check at the end of the iteration and the linear system of the next Newton iteration therefore used the residual of the rejected step instead of the accepted one, which could make the next Newton step inconsistent and cause the residual to blow up. The right-hand side is now reassembled at the accepted solution after the fallback. [#2186](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2186)
