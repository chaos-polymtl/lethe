## [Master] - 2026/11/10

### Changed

- MINOR This PR speeds up the assembly of the time-harmonic Maxwell solver. The local DPG system is now assembled from a unique base functions of the finite elements, which are shared by the real and imaginary parts of the electric and magnetic fields: the integral of each pair of base functions is computed once and added to the 2x2 block of the real and imaginary parts of the pair with the corresponding relevant coefficient (1, i, or -i), the Hermitian symmetry of the Gram matrix is used to integrate only half of the pairs (but the whole matrix is builded), and the face terms only involve the base functions whose tangential trace does not vanish on the face. The static condensation now uses Cholesky factorizations of the Gram matrix and of the interior condensation matrix instead of their explicit inverses. [#2207](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2207)

### Fixed

- MAJOR Following the Reviewer 1 comments of the DPG article, we notice that the test norm was not constraining enough the robin boundary term of the time-harmonic Maxwell solver (`silver_muller`, `impedance_boundary` and `waveguide_port` boundary conditions). They used the full trace of the electric test function, normal component included, instead of its tangential trace assuming that the use of Nedelec element (H(curl) space) was enough. This is not the case and now we ensure that only the tangential part of the test space is the only one appearing in the Robin boundary condition. Similarly, only the tangential component of the excitation of the `impedance_boundary` condition is now used, since the left-hand side of the Robin boundary condition is tangential. This changes the results of the simulations with Robin boundary conditions. [#2207](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2207)
