## [Master] - 2026/10/31

### Added

- MAJOR This PR adds the `sdirk45` time-stepping method, the 4th-order, 5-stage, L-stable and stiffly-accurate SDIRK4 method of Hairer and Wanner. [#2192](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2192)

### Removed

- MAJOR This PR removes the undocumented `sdirk43` time-stepping method. This 3-stage method was only A-stable and was not stiffly accurate, which made the pressure at the end of a time step depend on the pressure of the previous time step, and some of its stages lay outside the time step. Use `sdirk45` for 4th-order SDIRK time integration instead. The unused `core/sdirk.h` header and its source file are also removed. [#2192](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2192)

### Fixed

- MAJOR This PR fixes the SDIRK time-stepping schemes of the Navier-Stokes solvers. The stage times of the Butcher tableau were never used: the source term and the time-dependent boundary conditions were evaluated at the end of the time step for every stage, which reduced the schemes to first order for problems with a time-dependent forcing or boundary conditions. Each stage is now solved with the source term and the boundary conditions evaluated at its stage time. The auxiliary physics are now solved once per time step instead of once per stage. The coefficients of `sdirk33` are now computed to machine precision instead of 10 digits. The SDIRK schemes now throw an explicit error with the solvers that silently ignored the time derivative (block, VANS, CFD-DEM, sharp-interface immersed boundary, multiphase and variable-density flows) and with the mortar method. [#2192](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2192)
