## [Master] - 2026/11/25

### Fixed

- MAJOR The `pressure` boundary condition now imposes the normal traction $-p_{bc}\mathbf{n}$, where $p_{bc}$ is the function of the `p` subsection. The matrix-based and matrix-free solvers added a viscous term $-\nu \nabla \mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{n}$ to the prescribed traction, so the boundary condition imposed $p = p_{bc} + 2\nu\, \partial u_n / \partial n$ in the normal direction instead of the traction condition $\nu \nabla \mathbf{u} \cdot \mathbf{n} - p \mathbf{n} = -p_{bc} \mathbf{n}$. The results only change where the normal velocity gradient does not vanish on the pressure boundaries. The matrix-free solver also accumulates the update flags of the boundary faces, so that a `function_weak` boundary keeps the gradients it needs when it is combined with other boundary conditions with face terms. A new application test, `pressure_stagnation_flow`, checks the boundary condition with the matrix-based and matrix-free solvers. [#2241](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2241)
