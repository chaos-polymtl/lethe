## [Master] - 2026/11/16

### Added

- MAJOR This PR adds the calculation of the electromagnetic powers of the time-harmonic Maxwell solver, enabled with the new `calculate electromagnetic power` parameter of the `post-processing` subsection, and written in the file set by the new `electromagnetic power name` parameter. After each solve, the incident, outgoing and cross powers are computed for each Robin boundary (`silver_muller`, `impedance_boundary` and `waveguide_port`) by splitting the tangential electric field into an incident and an outgoing wave, the net Poynting flux is computed for each other boundary (`pec`, `pmc`, `electric_field` and `magnetic_field`), and the absorbed power is computed in each material from the imaginary parts of the permittivity and permeability and from the conductivity. The powers are in W when an `electromagnetic scaling type` is used, and non-dimensional otherwise. With `verbosity = verbose`, a summary of the power balance (incident, reflected, transmitted, cross, imposed and absorbed powers) is also printed, with its imbalance, which vanishes for the exact solution and therefore measures the numerical error (discretization and linear solver tolerance). A `hermitian_product` function is added to the tensor helpers. [#2215](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2215)

### Changed

- MINOR The Cuthill-McKee renumbering of the test and interior degrees of freedom of the time-harmonic Maxwell solver is removed. These degrees of freedom are only used locally on each cell, so their numbering is irrelevant, but the renumbering built the full sparsity pattern of their DoFHandler, which created a peak of the memory consumption of the solver and limited the size of the problems that could be solved. Only the skeleton degrees of freedom are still renumbered. [#2215](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2215)

### Fixed

- MINOR As noticed by the reviewer of the DPG article, the magnetic trace degrees of freedom of the time-harmonic Maxwell solver located only on Robin faces (inside a Robin face, or on an edge shared only by Robin faces) are not seen by any test function, since the magnetic trace is replaced by the Robin boundary condition on these faces, so they formed a nullspace of the condensed system. They are now constrained to zero. [#2215](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2215)

- MINOR The time-harmonic Maxwell solver failed with `electromagnetics trial degree = 0` (lowest-order case), since the mapping was created with the degree of the trial space. The degree of the mapping is now at least 1, and the theory documentation explains the relation between the polynomial index of the DPG formulation and the degree parameters of the `FEM` subsection. [#2215](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2215)

- MINOR The memory diagnostics of the time-harmonic Maxwell solver, printed with `verbosity = extra_verbose` in the electromagnetics linear solver subsection, computed the memory of the sparsity pattern from its global number of entries on every process, which overestimated it by the number of processes. The local number of entries is now used. [#2215](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2215)
