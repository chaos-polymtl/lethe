## [Master] - 2026/10/18

### Fixed

- MINOR Several Navier-Stokes assemblers of lethe-fluid assembled a Jacobian that did not match the derivative of their residual. The non-Newtonian CLS core multiplied the term (∇u)δu of the convection Jacobian by 0.5. The isothermal compressible core omitted the density in the Jacobian of the PSPG term and in the convective term of its strong Jacobian. The BDF assemblers of the isothermal compressible formulations (single-phase and CLS) did not multiply the pressure columns by the pressure scaling factor. The thermal buoyancy (single-phase and CLS), surface tension, Marangoni and evaporation recoil pressure assemblers only added their force to the strong residual when assembling the right-hand side, so the SUPG term of the Jacobian used a strong residual that differed from the one of the residual. These fixes only change the Jacobian; the converged solutions are unchanged. [#XXXX](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/XXXX)
