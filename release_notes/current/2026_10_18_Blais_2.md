## [Master] - 2026/10/18

### Fixed

- MINOR Several Navier-Stokes assemblers of lethe-fluid assembled a Jacobian that did not match the derivative of their residual. The non-Newtonian CLS core multiplied the term (∇u)δu of the convection Jacobian by 0.5. The isothermal compressible core omitted the density in the Jacobian of the PSPG term and in the convective term of its strong Jacobian. The thermal buoyancy (single-phase and CLS), surface tension, Marangoni and evaporation recoil pressure assemblers only added their force to the strong residual when assembling the right-hand side, so the SUPG term of the Jacobian used a strong residual that differed from the one of the residual. These fixes only change the Jacobian, so converged solutions are unchanged, but Newton converges faster. The heat_transfer_weakly_compressible_flow test changes because its Newton solver previously stopped 1.4% short of the hydrostatic pressure drop; it now matches it. [#2174](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2174)

- MAJOR This PR prevents the `pressure scaling factor` parameter of the `stabilization` subsection from being used with a non-constant density model (`isothermal_ideal_gas`) in `lethe-fluid` and `lethe-fluid-block`. The BDF assemblers of the isothermal compressible formulations (single-phase and CLS) did not multiply the pressure columns of the Jacobian by the factor, but the pressure degrees of freedom of the Newton correction were still rescaled at the end of the linear solve. This silently produced a wrong Newton correction. This combination was not tested, and the pressure enters the compressible Jacobian through many more terms than the incompressible one, so the factor is removed from the isothermal compressible assemblers and an explicit error is now thrown instead. [#2174](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2174)
