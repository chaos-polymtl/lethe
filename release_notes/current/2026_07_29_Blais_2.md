## [Master] - 2026/07/29

### Fixed

- MAJOR The time-averaged velocity and the Reynolds stresses were silently lost by the CFD-DEM solvers (`lethe-fluid-particles` and `lethe-fluid-particles-matrix-free`) in two situations. First, `CFDDEMSolver::load_balance` and `CFDDEMMatrixFree::load_balance` transferred the fluid solutions, the void fraction and the particle handler to the new partition, but not the averaging accumulators. The `setup_dofs` call that follows the repartitioning reinitializes those vectors, so the accumulated sums were reset at every load balance while `real_initial_time` was left untouched. The averaging numerator therefore only covered the time elapsed since the last load balance whereas the denominator still spanned the whole averaging window, and the average velocity and Reynolds stresses decayed towards zero as the simulation progressed. The two `load_balance` implementations now transfer the averaging vectors with `AverageVelocities::prepare_for_mesh_adaptation` and `AverageVelocities::post_mesh_adaptation`, as the mesh adaptation of the other fluid solvers already did. Second, `CFDDEMSolver::read_checkpoint` and `CFDDEMMatrixFree::read_checkpoint` duplicate the restart logic of `NavierStokesBase` and were never updated when `AverageVelocities::read` started deserializing into the vectors with ghost cells. The locally owned sum vectors, into which the averaging routines accumulate, were consequently left at zero after a restart, with the same effect on the averages, and the corrupted state was propagated to every subsequent checkpoint. The shared logic is now factored into `NavierStokesBase::restore_average_velocities_after_checkpoint`, which is called by the three `read_checkpoint` implementations. This also gives the CFD-DEM solvers the reinitialization of the average when the initial averaging time has not been reached yet, which was previously only available in the other fluid solvers. Finally, the condition deciding whether the average velocity vectors must be preserved is now expressed by `NavierStokesBase::average_velocities_are_enabled` so that it stays identical everywhere those vectors are serialized, deserialized or transferred.
