## [Master] - 2026/07/29

### Fixed

- MAJOR The time-averaged velocity and the Reynolds stresses were silently lost by the CFD-DEM solvers (`lethe-fluid-particles` and `lethe-fluid-particles-matrix-free`) in two situations. First, `CFDDEMSolver::load_balance` and `CFDDEMMatrixFree::load_balance` transferred the fluid solutions, the void fraction and the particle handler to the new partition, but not the averaging accumulators. The `setup_dofs` call that follows the repartitioning reinitializes those vectors, so the accumulated sums were reset at every load balance while `real_initial_time` was left untouched. The averaging numerator therefore only covered the time elapsed since the last load balance whereas the denominator still spanned the whole averaging window, and the average velocity and Reynolds stresses decayed towards zero as the simulation progressed. The two `load_balance` implementations now transfer the averaging vectors with `AverageVelocities::prepare_for_mesh_adaptation` and `AverageVelocities::post_mesh_adaptation`, as the mesh adaptation of the other fluid solvers already did. Second, `CFDDEMSolver::read_checkpoint` and `CFDDEMMatrixFree::read_checkpoint` duplicate the restart logic of `NavierStokesBase` and were never updated when `AverageVelocities::read` started deserializing into the vectors with ghost cells. The locally owned sum vectors, into which the averaging routines accumulate, were consequently left at zero after a restart, with the same effect on the averages, and the corrupted state was propagated to every subsequent checkpoint. The shared logic is now factored into `NavierStokesBase::restore_average_velocities_after_checkpoint`, which is called by the three `read_checkpoint` implementations. This also gives the CFD-DEM solvers the reinitialization of the average when the initial averaging time has not been reached yet, which was previously only available in the other fluid solvers. The condition deciding whether the average velocity vectors must be preserved is now expressed by `NavierStokesBase::average_velocities_are_enabled` so that it stays identical everywhere those vectors are serialized, deserialized or transferred. Finally, restarting a simulation used to accumulate one spurious contribution into the Reynolds stresses. `NavierStokesBase::read_checkpoint` called `calculate_average_velocities` once after the deserialization to give the averaged fields a meaningful value, but at that point `local_evaluation_point` had been zeroed by `setup_dofs` and is not restored from the checkpoint, so a fluctuation equal to the full average velocity was added to the Reynolds stress sums at every restart. The averaged fields are now reevaluated from the checkpointed sums instead: `AverageVelocities::sanitize_after_restart` takes the restart time as an argument, restores the total averaging time from the checkpointed start time of the averaging, and calls `AverageVelocities::update_average_velocities`. The averaged fields of a restarted simulation are consequently identical to those of an uninterrupted one, which is verified by the new `average_velocities_04` and `average_velocities_05` unit tests. Those two tests carry out the same time steps, the first one interrupting them with a mesh refinement and the second one with a restart, and are expected to print the same values.
