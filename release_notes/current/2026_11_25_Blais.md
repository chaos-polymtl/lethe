## [Master] - 2026/11/25

### Changed

- MINOR The report of the validation (`contrib/validation/validate_lethe.sh`) is assembled by the new script `generate_validation_report.py` instead of ImageMagick. It has a cover page, a clickable table of contents and bookmarks grouped by solver, and a title page before every case that lists its files and those that are missing. The plots are no longer rasterized: they remain vector graphics, scaled onto uniform pages under a header that names the case, and the report is about 15 times smaller. The validate.sh script of every case lists its plots with the new `add_to_report` function, the report is updated after every case, and the cases of `validation_cases*.txt` are grouped by solver, which is given in a third column. The validation now requires the Python package pypdf instead of ImageMagick. [#XXXX](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/XXXX)
