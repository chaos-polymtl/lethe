## [Master] - 2026/11/22

### Fixed

- MAJOR Contacts between non-spherical particles, or between them and walls, could be missed in the sharp immersed-boundary solver, for several reasons. The bounding-box check of the walls was inverted in 2D, so non-spherical particles never felt a wall. The bounding-box check between particles used the rows of the rotation matrices instead of their columns, and wrong cross-product axes in 3D. A floating wall was only detected close to the point which defines it. Finally, when the bounding boxes did not touch, the origin of the domain was stored as the initial guess of the next search of the contact point, and the particle-particle and particle-wall searches overwrote the guesses of each other. This changes the results of the cases with contacts of non-spherical particles. The contacts of the `death_star`, `cone`, `cylindrical_tube`, `cut_hollow_sphere` and `rbf` shapes, and of composites made of them, remain unreliable since their bounding box does not match their level set.
