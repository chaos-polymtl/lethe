## [Master] - 2026/07/27

### Changed

- MINOR Fixed the compilation warnings introduced by the deal.II 9.8 release. The deprecated `MGTransferGlobalCoarsening<dim, VectorType>` alias used by the matrix-free solvers (`lethe-fluid-matrix-free` and `lethe-fluid-particles-matrix-free`) is replaced by `MGTransferMatrixFree<dim, Number>`, into which it was merged upstream. The implicit `double`-to-`unsigned int` conversions in `insertion_volume.cc` are made explicit (redundant `floor` calls on integer division removed, and the point-count truncation wrapped in a `static_cast`). Finally, the deal.II-internal `-Wdeprecated-declarations` warning triggered when instantiating `SparseMatrixTools::restrict_to_full_matrices` (which still relies on the deprecated `ConsensusAlgorithms::Process` class internally) is suppressed locally at the call site until it is resolved upstream. [#2071](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2071)
