## [Master] - 2026/10/15

### Added

- MAJOR This PR adds the skeleton of the solid phase auxiliary physics of the Euler-Euler (two-fluid) model, coupled to the volume-averaged Navier-Stokes (VANS) solver through the multiphysics interface. It adds the `solid phase` parameter in the `multiphysics` subsection, the `solid phase degree` parameter in the `FEM` subsection, and `solid phase` subsections in the `linear solver` and `non-linear solver` subsections. The solid phase fields are allocated, written to the output files and checkpointed, but their equations are not solved yet. [#2169](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2169)
