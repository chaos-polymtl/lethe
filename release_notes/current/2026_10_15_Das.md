## [Master] - 2026/10/15

### Added

- MAJOR This PR adds the skeleton of the continuum solid dynamics auxiliary physics, which holds the solid phase of the Euler-Euler (two-fluid) model and is coupled to the volume-averaged Navier-Stokes (VANS) solver through the multiphysics interface. It adds the `continuum solid dynamics` parameter in the `multiphysics` subsection, the `solid velocity degree` and `solid volume fraction degree` parameters in the `FEM` subsection, and `continuum solid dynamics` subsections in the `linear solver` and `non-linear solver` subsections. The solid phase fields are allocated, written to the output files and checkpointed, but their equations are not solved yet. [#2169](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2169)
