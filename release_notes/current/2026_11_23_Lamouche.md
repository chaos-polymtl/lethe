## [Master] - 2026/11/23

### Added

- MAJOR The new parameter `checkpoint control` of subsection restart sets what triggers the checkpoints, like `output control` for the results: `iteration` (default, every `frequency` iterations), `time` (every `time frequency` of simulated time) or `wall_time` (every `wall time frequency` seconds of elapsed wall time). The `wall_time` control bounds the computing time lost when a job is killed, without having to estimate the duration of an iteration. [#2236](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2236)
