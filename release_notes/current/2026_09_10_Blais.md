## [Master] - 2026/09/10

### Fixed

- MAJOR This PR prevents the `pressure scaling factor` parameter of the `stabilization` subsection from being used with the matrix-free solvers (`lethe-fluid-matrix-free`, `lethe-fluid-vans-matrix-free` and `lethe-fluid-particles-matrix-free`). The matrix-free operators never scaled the pressure trial functions, so the factor was not applied to the Jacobian, but the pressure degrees of freedom of the Newton correction were still rescaled at the end of the linear solve. This silently produced a wrong Newton correction. An explicit error is now thrown instead. [#PR](link)
