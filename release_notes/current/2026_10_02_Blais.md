## [Master] - 2026/10/02

### Changed

- MINOR This PR speeds up the assembly of the discontinuous Galerkin (DG) formulation of the tracer physics. The hessians, laplacians and previous gradients, which are only used by the stabilization of the continuous formulation, are no longer computed when DG is used. The shape function jumps, averages and upwind values at the internal faces are now gathered once per quadrature point instead of inside the (i, j) loops of the SIPG assembler. The boundary condition type is no longer tested in the innermost loop of the boundary Nitsche assembler, and the face arrays are allocated once instead of being resized at every face. The results are unchanged. [#2145](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2145/)

### Fixed

- MINOR The DG formulation of the tracer physics threw "No valid boundary conditions types were identified." when a boundary used the `none` tracer boundary condition, or had no tracer boundary condition at all. These boundaries now impose the natural boundary condition of the DG formulation, which is a zero total (advective and diffusive) flux, as expected for walls. [#2145](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2145/)
