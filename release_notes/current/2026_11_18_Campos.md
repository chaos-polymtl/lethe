## [Master] - 2026/11/18

### Added

- MAJOR This PR adds the evolution in phase space of the moments of the quadrature method of moments (QMOM) auxiliary physics, with QMOMKit. At every degree of freedom, the moments are inverted into a quadrature with the Wheeler algorithm, the quadrature is evolved over the time step and the moments are rebuilt from it. The growth, nucleation and coalescence of the particles are enabled with `enable growth`, `enable nucleation` and `enable coalescence`, and defined in the new `growth` (`constant`, `linear` or `function` model), `nucleation` (`constant` or `function` rate, log-normal distribution of the nuclei sizes) and `coalescence` (`constant` or `function` kernel, conserving the volume of the particles) subsections of the `QMOM` subsection. These mechanisms require the `bdf1` time-stepping method. The new `relative tolerance` and `absolute tolerance` parameters of the `phase evolution` subsection control the integration of the trajectories of the quadrature nodes. The simulation stops with an error if the moments of a degree of freedom are not realizable. The `number of moments` must now be even. The L2 errors of the QMOM fields are now relative to the L2 norm of their analytical solution. [#2223](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2223)
