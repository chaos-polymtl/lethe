## [Master] - 2026/11/18

### Added

- MAJOR This PR adds the evolution in phase space of the moments of the quadrature method of moments (QMOM) auxiliary physics, with QMOMKit. At every degree of freedom, the moments are inverted into a quadrature with the Wheeler algorithm, the quadrature is evolved over the time step and the moments are rebuilt from it. The growth of the particles is enabled with `enable growth` and defined in the new `growth` subsection of the `QMOM` subsection, with a `constant`, `linear` or `function` model. The new `relative tolerance` and `absolute tolerance` parameters of the `phase evolution` subsection control the integration of the trajectories of the quadrature nodes. The nucleation and the coalescence are not implemented yet. The simulation stops with an error if the moments of a degree of freedom are not realizable.
