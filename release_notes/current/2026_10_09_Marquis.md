## [Master] - 2026/10/09

### Changed

- MAJOR This PR refactors the assembly of the time-harmonic Maxwell solver to follow the architecture of the other solvers: a `TimeHarmonicMaxwellScratchData`, cell and face assemblers (`TimeHarmonicMaxwellAssemblerCore`, `TimeHarmonicMaxwellAssemblerSkeleton` and `TimeHarmonicMaxwellAssemblerRobinBC`), a `DPGCopyData` and a `WorkStream` loop. The same local DPG system is now shared by the assembly of the skeleton system and by the reconstruction of the interior solution, which removes the duplicated assembly code.
