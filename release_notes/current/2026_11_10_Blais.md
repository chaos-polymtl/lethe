## [Master] - 2026/11/10

### Fixed

- MINOR This PR fixes the restart of the matrix-free fluid dynamics solver, and of every fluid dynamics solver when Lethe is built with deal.II vectors (`LETHE_USE_LDV`). The solution vectors read from the checkpoint were left without their ghost values until the first solve of the restarted simulation. On the first restarted time step, the dynamic flow control therefore calculated the flow rate at its inlet with zeros on the degrees of freedom shared with another process, and applied a spurious beta force: in a turbulent channel flow restarted on 128 processes, it read an average velocity of 0.97 instead of 1 and applied a beta force about 700 times larger than its equilibrium value. The ghost values of the present and previous solutions are now updated when they are read from the checkpoint, as was already done by the matrix-free CFD-DEM solver. [#2206](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2206)
