## [Master] - 2026/10/18

### Added

- MINOR Ten examples were added to the automatic validation of Lethe on Lucille (`contrib/validation/validation_cases_lucille.txt`). They each run in a few minutes and compare against an analytical solution, a correlation or experimental data: the Taylor-Couette flow with a Nitsche immersed boundary (exact torque), the Stefan problem, the Rayleigh-Bénard convection at Ra=10^4 (Ouertatani et al.), the concentric heat exchanger (conjugate heat transfer, NTU method), the air bubble compression and the water injection in a closed cell (compressible CLS), the capillary rise following Jurin's law (CHNS with a contact angle), the heated packed bed (DEM with heat transfer, Beaulieu experiments), the cylindrical packed bed (Ergun correlation) and the single particle sedimentation (unresolved CFD-DEM). Every example has a `validate.sh` script, and its post-processing script has a `--validate` option that stores the plotted data in `.dat` files and saves the figures as PDF without displaying them. A new post-processing script, `postprocess_taylor_couette_nitsche.py`, plots the torques and the velocity error of the Taylor-Couette flow with a Nitsche immersed boundary.
