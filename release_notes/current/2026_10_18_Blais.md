## [Master] - 2026/10/18

### Added

- MINOR Ten examples were added to the automatic validation of Lethe on Lucille (`contrib/validation/validation_cases_lucille.txt`). They each run in a few minutes and compare against an analytical solution, a correlation or experimental data: the Taylor-Couette flow with a Nitsche immersed boundary (exact torque), the Stefan problem, the Rayleigh-Bénard convection at Ra=10^4 (Ouertatani et al.), the concentric heat exchanger (conjugate heat transfer, NTU method), the air bubble compression and the water injection in a closed cell (compressible CLS), the capillary rise following Jurin's law (CHNS with a contact angle), the heated packed bed (DEM with heat transfer, Beaulieu experiments), the cylindrical packed bed (Ergun correlation) and the single particle sedimentation (unresolved CFD-DEM). Every example has a `validate.sh` script, and its post-processing script has a `--validate` option that stores the plotted data in `.dat` files and saves the figures as PDF without displaying them. A new post-processing script, `postprocess_taylor_couette_nitsche.py`, plots the torques and the velocity error of the Taylor-Couette flow with a Nitsche immersed boundary. The two parameter files of the Rayleigh-Bénard convection example now write their results in separate folders (`output_10k` and `output_1M`), so that both cases can be postprocessed together without renaming the output folder. [#2172](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2172)
### Fixed

- MAJOR The void fraction was never percolated at the end of a time step in `lethe-fluid-vans-matrix-free`, since the solver called `finish_time_step()` instead of `finish_time_step_fd()`. The time derivative of the void fraction was therefore always evaluated with respect to the initial void fraction. Only transient simulations with a void fraction that varies in time (e.g., a time-dependent void fraction function) and `void fraction time derivative = true` are affected. `lethe-fluid-particles-matrix-free` was not affected. [#](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/)
- MINOR The Jacobian of the matrix-free VANS operator read the hessians of the trial functions when `enable hessians in residual` was true, but only evaluated them when `enable hessians in jacobian` was true. The multigrid level operators are always built with the hessians enabled in the residual, so setting `mg enable hessians in jacobian = false` made the level operators read hessians that were never evaluated (an assertion in debug, undefined values in release). The hessians are now read only when they are evaluated. This only affects the preconditioner, not the converged solution. [#](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/)
