## [Master] - 2026/09/30

### Changed

- MINOR This PR refactors the assembly of the time-harmonic Maxwell solver to follow the architecture of the other solvers: a `TimeHarmonicMaxwellScratchData`, cell and face assemblers (`TimeHarmonicMaxwellAssemblerCore`, `TimeHarmonicMaxwellAssemblerSkeleton` and `TimeHarmonicMaxwellAssemblerRobinBC`), a `DPGCopyData` and a `WorkStream` loop. The same local DPG system is now shared by the assembly of the skeleton system and by the reconstruction of the interior solution, which removes the duplicated assembly code. The results are unchanged. [#PR](link)

### Fixed

- MINOR The system matrix and right-hand side of the time-harmonic Maxwell solver were not reset before their assembly. When the time-harmonic Maxwell equations were solved at more than one coupling step on the same mesh, the new system was added to the previous ones, which gave wrong results when the electromagnetic properties depend on the temperature. [#PR](link)
