## [Master] - 2026/11/20

### Changed

- MINOR The matrix-free Navier-Stokes solver and its geometric multigrid preconditioner are significantly faster and use less memory. The 3D Taylor-Green vortex (Q2, 1.1M DoFs) and Taylor-Couette (Q2, 5.6M DoFs) matrix-free examples run 38 to 46% faster on 16 cores, and their peak memory drops by 35 to 55%. The changes are the following. [#XXXX](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/XXXX)
  - The operators no longer carry out work whose result is never used. The quadrature-point tables of the linearization point are only allocated and filled when the operator reads them: the hessian only when it is enabled in the Jacobian, the full hessian only for the VANS operator, the non-Newtonian tables only for non-Newtonian flows, the advective velocity only with mortar and the LSIC parameter only with GLS. The table of the element sizes is no longer copied for every cell batch, and the time-stepping data and the viscosity are gathered once per application of the operator instead of once per cell batch. The hessians of the test functions are only integrated for the GLS term, the only term that produces them, while the hessians of the solution are still evaluated whenever they are enabled, since the Laplacian of the velocity appears in the PSPG and SUPG terms. The MatrixFree objects only store the second derivatives of the mapping and the quadrature points when they are used, and the multigrid levels no longer request the hessians of the residual, which they never evaluate.
  - The coarse-grid matrix is assembled once per setup of the preconditioner instead of twice with the GMRES coarse-grid solver, and the temperature transfer of the thermal-buoyancy multigrid is built once per mesh instead of at every Newton iteration.
  - The quadrature-point terms of the Newtonian and VANS operators are factorized. The Jacobian computes the strong residual linearized around the previous Newton iterate and the strong residual of that iterate once per quadrature point and reuses them in the PSPG, SUPG and GLS terms, instead of recomputing them for every pair of velocity components. The residual does the same with its strong residual. This changes the operators only up to round-off and makes the computation of the diagonal of the multigrid smoother 25 to 30% faster for the Navier-Stokes operator and 17% faster for the VANS operator.
  - The Cuthill-McKee renumbering is no longer applied to the degrees of freedom of the solver and of the levels of the global coarsening multigrid, except on the coarsest level, which is used by the matrix-based coarse-grid solvers. The renumbering does not benefit the matrix-free operators and it was the most expensive and most memory-consuming step of the setup. The local smoothing multigrid and the ILU preconditioner keep it. The numbering changes the initial vector of the eigenvalue estimation of the multigrid smoother, so the estimated eigenvalues change by about 1% and the solutions change within the tolerance of the linear solver. The outputs of 13 matrix-free application tests were regenerated for this reason.

### Fixed

- MINOR The Jacobian of the outlet boundary condition of the matrix-free Navier-Stokes operator read the stored values of the previous Newton iterate with the index of the face batch instead of its index among the boundary face batches, which is the index with which the table is written. Both indices are currently equal, since the matrix-free operators do not set up inner faces, so the results are unchanged. A unit test compares the outlet term of the matrix-free Jacobian with a direct evaluation. [#XXXX](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/XXXX)
