## [Master] - 2026/10/14

### Fixed

- MAJOR The time derivative of the void fraction was counted `dim` times in the residual of the matrix-free VANS operator (`lethe-fluid-vans-matrix-free` and `lethe-fluid-particles-matrix-free`), both in the continuity equation and in the grad-div stabilization term, because it was added inside the loops over the velocity components. The continuity equation solved was therefore dim·∂ɛ/∂t + ∇·(ɛu) = 0 instead of ∂ɛ/∂t + ∇·(ɛu) = 0. The term is now added once, as in the matrix-based VANS assemblers. Only simulations with `void fraction time derivative = true` (the default value of this parameter) and a void fraction that varies in time are affected. No existing test was affected, since all matrix-free tests either disable this parameter or have a void fraction that is constant in time. On a single particle sedimentation case, the effect of the void fraction time derivative on the particle velocity now matches the one obtained with `lethe-fluid-particles`, whereas it was three times larger before. [#2168](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2168)
- MINOR The drag term βu was counted twice in the SUPG part of the Jacobian of the matrix-free VANS operator, since it is already included in the strong residual through the particle-fluid source term. This only affects the Jacobian, and thus the convergence of the Newton solver, not the converged solution. The reference output of the `qcm_packed_bed_rong_drag_implicit_gcmg.mpirun=2` test changes in the last printed digit of the pressure drop. [#2168](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2168)
