## [Master] - 2026/10/07

### Added

- MAJOR A new `kuzmin` option was added to the `scalar limiter` parameter of the `stabilization` subsection. It applies the hierarchical vertex-based limiter of Kuzmin (2010) to the discontinuous Galerkin (DG) formulation of the tracer physics. The linear part of the solution and its higher-order part are rescaled separately within each cell, using bounds established at the vertices from the cells that share them, and the linear part is never limited more than the higher-order part. Contrary to the `moe` limiter, this limiter consequently preserves the smooth extrema of the solution when the tracer degree is at least 2. It does not have any parameter and it conserves the tracer.
