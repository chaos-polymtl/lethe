## [Master] - 2026/11/15

### Added

- MINOR This PR adds the 2D backward-facing step to the automatic validation suite, for ten Reynolds numbers between 100 and 1000 (reattachment length compared with the benchmark of Erturk (2008)). [#2212](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2212)

### Changed

- MINOR The post-processing script of the backward-facing step example now computes the reattachment length from the velocity gradient written by Lethe on the bottom wall, instead of a bisection on finite differences of the velocity near the wall. [#2212](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2212)
- MINOR The backward-facing step example now imposes a Poiseuille velocity profile at the inlet instead of a uniform velocity, which did not have the length to develop before the step at high Reynolds numbers, and obtains the steady solution at every Reynolds number by a continuation on the kinematic viscosity. The parameter file at Re = 1000, which used pseudo time stepping, is replaced by a template and scripts that generate, launch and post-process a sweep on the Reynolds number between 100 and 1000, and the script comparing the velocity profile at the outlet with a Poiseuille flow is removed. The reattachment length agrees with the benchmark of Erturk (2008) within 1.8% for every Reynolds number, against up to 4.4% with the uniform inlet velocity. [#2212](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2212)
