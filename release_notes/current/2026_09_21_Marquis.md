## [Master] - 2026/09/21

### Added

- MAJOR The matrix-based VANS and CFD-DEM solvers (`lethe-fluid-vans` and `lethe-fluid-particles`) now solve the auxiliary physics that are enabled in the parameter file. Additionally, the `MultiphysicsInterface` now knows whether the fluid dynamics solver driving it solves the standard or the volume-averaged (VANS) Navier-Stokes equations. Heat transfer, tracer, CLS and Cahn-Hilliard all consume the fluid velocity in their governing equation, and none of them has a volume-averaged form implemented yet; enabling one of them together with a VANS or CFD-DEM solver (`lethe-fluid-vans`, `lethe-fluid-vans-matrix-free`, `lethe-fluid-particles` and `lethe-fluid-particles-matrix-free`) now throws an error at startup instead of solving the non-volume-averaged equation. Electromagnetics is exempt from this restriction, since the time-harmonic Maxwell equations do not depend on the fluid velocity or the void fraction and its existing implementation is therefore already correct under a VANS/CFD-DEM solver (only the material properties would be dependent on the void fraction in a CFD-DEM case).
