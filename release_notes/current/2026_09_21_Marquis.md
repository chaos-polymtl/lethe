## [Master] - 2026/09/21

### Added

- MAJOR The matrix-based VANS and CFD-DEM solvers (`lethe-fluid-vans` and `lethe-fluid-particles`) now solve the auxiliary physics that are enabled in the parameter file. Additionally, the `MultiphysicsInterface` now knows whether the fluid dynamics solver driving it solves the standard or the volume-averaged (VANS) Navier-Stokes equations. Heat transfer, tracer, CLS and Cahn-Hilliard all consume the fluid velocity in their governing equation, and none of them has a volume-averaged form implemented yet; enabling one of them together with a VANS or CFD-DEM solver (`lethe-fluid-vans`, `lethe-fluid-vans-matrix-free`, `lethe-fluid-particles` and `lethe-fluid-particles-matrix-free`) now throws an error at startup instead of solving the non-volume-averaged equation. Electromagnetics is exempt from this restriction, since the time-harmonic Maxwell equations do not depend on the fluid velocity or the void fraction and its existing implementation is therefore already correct under a VANS/CFD-DEM solver (only the material properties would be dependent of the void fraction in a CFD-DEM case). [#2133](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2133)
- MAJOR The CFD-DEM solvers were not able to perform mesh refinement because the particles container and the void fraction were never updated between refinements. However, the whole architecture was there, as it was needed to perform load balancing. This PR adds the possibility to perform mesh refinement, a feature that is used in the test for the electromagnetic solver used in a pure CFD-DEM simulation to recreate the convergence behaviour of the electromagnetic solve. [#2133](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/2133)
