## [Master] - 2026/09/21

### Added

- MAJOR The matrix-based VANS and CFD-DEM solvers (`lethe-fluid-vans` and `lethe-fluid-particles`) now solve the auxiliary physics that are enabled in the parameter file. Additionally, the `MultiphysicsInterface` now knows whether the fluid dynamics solver driving it solves the standard or the volume-averaged (VANS) Navier-Stokes equations. Heat transfer, tracer, CLS and Cahn-Hilliard all consume the fluid velocity in their governing equation, and none of them has a volume-averaged form implemented yet; enabling one of them together with a VANS or CFD-DEM solver (`lethe-fluid-vans`, `lethe-fluid-vans-matrix-free`, `lethe-fluid-particles` and `lethe-fluid-particles-matrix-free`) now throws an error at startup instead of solving the non-volume-averaged equation. Electromagnetics is already enabled in a VANS formulation as the. [#PR](link)

# Added
- MAJOR The CFD-DEM solvers were not able to perform mesh refinement because the particles container and the void fraction where never updated between refinement. However, the whole architecture was there as it needed to be done when doing load balancing. This PR also add the possibility to perform mesh refinement, a feature that is used in the test for the electromagnetic solver use in pure CFD-DEM simulation to recreate the convergence behaviour of the electromagnetic solve.
