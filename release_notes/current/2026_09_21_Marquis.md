## [Master] - 2026/09/21

### Added

- MAJOR `MultiphysicsInterface` now knows whether the fluid dynamics solver driving it solves the standard or the volume-averaged (VANS) Navier-Stokes equations. Heat transfer, tracer, CLS and Cahn-Hilliard all consume the fluid velocity in their governing equation, and none of them has a volume-averaged form implemented yet; enabling one of them together with a VANS or CFD-DEM solver (`lethe-fluid-vans`, `lethe-fluid-vans-matrix-free`, `lethe-fluid-particles` and its matrix-free counterpart) now throws a clear error at startup instead of silently solving the wrong, non-volume-averaged equation. Electromagnetics is unaffected, since the time-harmonic Maxwell equations do not depend on the fluid velocity or the void fraction. [#PR](link)

### Fixed

- MAJOR The matrix-based VANS and CFD-DEM solvers (`lethe-fluid-vans` and `lethe-fluid-particles`) now solve the auxiliary physics that are enabled in the parameter file. `FluidDynamicsVANS::iterate()` overrode `NavierStokesBase::iterate()` without calling `multiphysics->solve()`, so an auxiliary physics enabled in a VANS or CFD-DEM simulation was allocated, post-processed, written to the output files and checkpointed, but never solved, and no warning was issued. The matrix-free solvers were not affected since they do not override `iterate()`. The same two `solve()` functions also never called `update_multiphysics_time_average_solution()`, which left the time-averaged velocity field unavailable to the auxiliary physics when `calculate average velocities` was enabled or when the initial condition type was `average_velocity_profile`. The time-harmonic Maxwell equations and the fluid-phase heat transfer can now be used within the CFD-DEM solvers; two application tests were added for this purpose. Note that the fluid-phase heat transfer equation is not volume-averaged, so it is only exact when the void fraction is one, and that the fluid and the particles still do not exchange heat with one another.
