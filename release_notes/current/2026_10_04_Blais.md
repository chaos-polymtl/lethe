## [Master] - 2026/10/04

### Fixed

- MAJOR The discontinuous Galerkin (DG) formulation of the CLS produced spurious oscillations of the phase indicator. Three defects were corrected. First, the cell term was assembled in conservative form, so a uniform phase indicator was not preserved when the velocity field is not exactly divergence-free. The velocity divergence term is now assembled, as is done for the DG tracer, unless the CLS is compressible. Second, the cell term used the velocity extrapolated in time with the BDF scheme while the face terms used the non-extrapolated velocity. Both now use the extrapolated velocity. Third, no flux was assembled on the boundary faces, so the phase indicator could neither enter nor leave the domain and `dirichlet` boundary conditions were ignored. The upwinded boundary flux is now assembled for the `none` and `dirichlet` boundary conditions. This changes the results of simulations that use `cls uses dg = true`. [#](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/)

### Changed

- MAJOR When the CLS is solved with the discontinuous Galerkin (DG) formulation, the other physics and the projections of the phase indicator gradient and of the curvature now use a continuous representation of the phase indicator instead of the discontinuous one. It is obtained with a lumped-mass L2 projection of the DG solution, computed with a Gauss-Lobatto quadrature: the value at each degree of freedom is a weighted average of the values of the DG phase indicator in the cells that share it. This projection does not require the solution of a linear system, it preserves the integral of the phase indicator and it does not modify a phase indicator that is already continuous. This is required for the surface tension force, which was computed from the cell-wise gradient of the discontinuous phase indicator and thus ignored its jumps between the cells. The density and the viscosity used by the fluid dynamics are also computed from the continuous representation, which changes the results of simulations that use `cls uses dg = true`. [#](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/)
