## [Master] - 2026/10/04

### Fixed

- MAJOR The discontinuous Galerkin (DG) formulation of the CLS produced spurious oscillations of the phase indicator. Three defects were corrected. First, the cell term was assembled in conservative form, so a uniform phase indicator was not preserved when the velocity field is not exactly divergence-free. The velocity divergence term is now assembled, as is done for the DG tracer, unless the CLS is compressible. Second, the cell term used the velocity extrapolated in time with the BDF scheme while the face terms used the non-extrapolated velocity. Both now use the extrapolated velocity. Third, no flux was assembled on the boundary faces, so the phase indicator could neither enter nor leave the domain and `dirichlet` boundary conditions were ignored. The upwinded boundary flux is now assembled for the `none` and `dirichlet` boundary conditions. This changes the results of simulations that use `cls uses dg = true`.
