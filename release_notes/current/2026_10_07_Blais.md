## [Master] - 2026/10/07

### Fixed

- MAJOR The `moe` scalar limiter of the discontinuous Galerkin (DG) formulation of the tracer physics had three defects. First, the solution was rescaled around the arithmetic average of the nodal values instead of the cell average. The two only coincide for a degree of one on affine cells, so the limiter did not conserve the tracer for higher degrees. The cell average is now obtained by integrating the solution over the cell. Second, the regularization of the ratio between the lower bound and the minimum of the cell had the wrong sign. When the variation of the solution within a cell was at the level of round-off, the limiting factor became a very large negative number and the limiter could amplify the solution instead of bounding it. The ratios are now only calculated when the variation is above round-off and the limiting factor is kept between zero and one. Third, the cells that share a vertex through a periodic boundary were not considered as neighbors. In addition, the extrema of the solution within a cell are now sampled at the quadrature points as well as at the support points, since the extrema of a solution of degree above one are not necessarily located at the support points. The results obtained with a degree of one are not expected to change, whereas those obtained with a higher degree do. [#XXXX](https://github.com/chaos-polymtl/lethe/pull/XXXX)
