Compute the excitation \(\mathbf{g}\) and the surface admittance \(Y\) of the Robin boundary condition \(\mathbf{n}\times\mathbf{H} + Y \mathbf{E}_T = \mathbf{g}\) at a point of a boundary with a Robin boundary condition (silver_muller, impedance_boundary or waveguide_port).

Template Parameters
dimNumber of spatial dimensions.
Parameters
[in]time_harmonic_maxwell_parametersParameters of the time-harmonic Maxwell physics, which contain the waveguide port definitions.
[in]boundary_conditionsBoundary conditions of the time-harmonic Maxwell physics.
[in]waveguide_ports_relative_amplitudesElectric field amplitude of each waveguide port divided by the maximum amplitude of all the ports. Only used on waveguide ports.
[in]boundary_idBoundary id of the face.
[in]positionPosition where the Robin boundary data is computed.
[in]normalUnit outward normal vector of the face at the position.
[in]effective_electric_permittivityEffective electric permittivity at the position.
[in]effective_magnetic_permeabilityEffective magnetic permeability at the position.
Returns
A pair containing the excitation and the surface admittance.

Compute the net, incident, outgoing and cross power densities (powers per unit area of the boundary) at a point of a boundary with a Robin boundary condition \(\mathbf{n}\times\mathbf{H} + Y\mathbf{E}_T = \mathbf{g}\).

Template Parameters
dimNumber of spatial dimensions.
Parameters
[in]E_TTangential electric field at the point.
[in]g_TTangential component of the excitation at the point.
[in]YSurface admittance at the point.
Returns
The power densities. If \(Y = 0\), the incident and outgoing waves are not defined, and the whole net power density is returned as cross power density.

Compute the excitation and the surface admittance of the Robin boundary condition of a rectangular waveguide port from the input parameters. The excitation used is the normalized one across all the waveguide ports. This function is only implemented for 3D problems.

Template Parameters
dimNumber of spatial dimensions.
Parameters
[in]time_harmonic_maxwell_parametersParameters of the time-harmonic Maxwell physics, which contain the waveguide port definitions.
[in]pPosition where the excitation is computed.
[in]normalUnit normal vector of the waveguide port face.
[in]effective_electric_permittivityEffective electric permittivity at the point p.
[in]effective_magnetic_permeabilityEffective magnetic permeability at the point p.
[in]waveguide_port_indexIndex of the waveguide port condition within the waveguide port parameters.
[in]relative_amplitudeElectric field amplitude of the port divided by the maximum amplitude of all the ports.
Returns
A pair containing the excitation of the Robin boundary condition and the surface admittance at the point p.
