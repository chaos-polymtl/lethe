Detailed Description

template<int dim>
class ContinuumSolidDynamics< dim >

Solid phase of the Euler-Euler model, solved as an auxiliary physics coupled to the VANS fluid dynamics solver.

Component layout of the FESystem: components [0, dim) hold the solid velocity and component dim holds the solid volume fraction.

Template Parameters
dimNumber of spatial dimensions.
