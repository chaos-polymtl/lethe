Detailed Description

template<int dim>
class TimeHarmonicMaxwellAssemblerRobinBC< dim >

Class that assembles the Robin boundary conditions of the time-harmonic Maxwell equations (silver_muller, impedance_boundary and waveguide_port). On these faces, the magnetic trace is expressed from the electric trace with the surface admittance \(Y\) and the excitation \(\mathbf{g}\), which gives the terms \(-\langle Y \hat{\mathbf{E}}, \mathbf{F} \rangle\) in \(\hat{B}\) and \(-\langle \mathbf{g}, \mathbf{F} \rangle\) in \(l\). Since the energy norm that is minimized is also modified on these faces, it also assembles the boundary terms of the Gram matrix \(G\):

\[\left(\mathbf{n}\times\mathbf{I}+Y*\mathbf{n}\times(\mathbf{F}\times\mathbf{n}), \mathbf{n}\times\tilde{\mathbf{I}}+Y*\mathbf{n}\times(\tilde{\mathbf{F}}\times\mathbf{n})\right)_{\Gamma_R}.\]

Compute the excitation and the surface admittance of the Robin boundary condition of a rectangular waveguide port from the input parameters. The excitation used is the normalized one across all the waveguide ports. This function is only implemented for 3D problems.

Parameters
[in]pPosition where the excitation is computed.
[in]normalUnit normal vector of the waveguide port face.
[in]effective_electric_permittivityEffective electric permittivity at the point p.
[in]effective_magnetic_permeabilityEffective magnetic permeability at the point p.
[in]waveguide_port_indexIndex of the waveguide port condition within the waveguide port parameters.
Returns
A pair containing the excitation of the Robin boundary condition and the surface admittance at the point p. The excitation is scaled by the ratio of the port amplitude to the maximum amplitude of all the ports.
