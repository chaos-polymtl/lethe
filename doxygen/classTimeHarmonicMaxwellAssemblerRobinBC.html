Detailed Description

template<int dim>
class TimeHarmonicMaxwellAssemblerRobinBC< dim >

Class that assembles the Robin boundary conditions of the time-harmonic Maxwell equations (silver_muller, impedance_boundary and waveguide_port). On these faces, the magnetic trace is expressed from the electric trace with the surface admittance \(Y\) and the excitation \(\mathbf{g}\), which gives the terms \(-\langle Y \hat{\mathbf{E}}, \mathbf{F} \rangle\) in \(\hat{B}\) and \(-\langle \mathbf{g}, \mathbf{F} \rangle\) in \(l\), where only the tangential component of \(\mathbf{g}\) is used since the left-hand side of the Robin boundary condition is tangential. Since the energy norm that is minimized is also modified on these faces, it also assembles the boundary terms of the Gram matrix \(G\):

\[\left(\mathbf{n}\times\mathbf{I}+Y^*\mathbf{n}\times(\mathbf{F}\times\mathbf{n}), \mathbf{n}\times\tilde{\mathbf{I}}+Y^*\mathbf{n}\times(\tilde{\mathbf{F}}\times\mathbf{n})\right)_{\Gamma_R},\]

where \(Y^*\) is the complex conjugate of \(Y\) and the inner product is conjugated in its first argument. Again, this term only involves the tangential traces of the test functions.
