Detailed Description

VelocitySource - Adds velocity-dependent source term to the Navier-Stokes equations with the appropriate jacobian matrix. Currently only a change to a rotating frame is supported, but additional terms such as a Darcy force or similar could be easily added.

◆ enable_darcy_multiply_by_density

bool Parameters::VelocitySource::enable_darcy_multiply_by_density = false

Enable the multiplication of the Darcy force term ( \(\vec{F}_\mathrm{Darcy}\)) by the density for dimensional consistency when solving the pressure rather than the kinematic pressure in the momentum balance.

\[\vec{F}_\mathrm{Darcy} = \rho K \vec{u} \]

with \(\rho\) the density, \(K\) the Darcy penalty, and \(\vec{u}\) the velocity.
