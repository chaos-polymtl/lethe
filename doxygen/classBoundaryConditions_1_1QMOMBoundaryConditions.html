Detailed Description

template<int dim>
class BoundaryConditions::QMOMBoundaryConditions< dim >

Boundary conditions of the QMOM physics. They apply to the advective flux of all the QMOM fields (scalars and moments), which is meant to be computed with an upwind numerical flux:

  • dirichlet: the prescribed values enter the domain where the flow enters (u.n < 0) and the interior values leave it where the flow leaves;
  • outlet: the interior values leave the domain where the flow leaves and nothing enters where the flow enters (backflow);
  • none: the advective flux is zero, which is the natural choice for walls. Boundaries without a QMOM boundary condition are treated as none.
