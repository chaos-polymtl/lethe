Detailed Description

template<int dim>
class QMOMScratchData< dim >

Scratch data of the transport of the QMOM components.

The transport is linear and is solved directly for the solution at the end of the time step (linear solver strategy). Unlike the tracer physics, which assembles the residual of a Newton iteration, the assembly therefore never needs the present solution: the matrix only needs the shape functions and the fluid velocity, and the right-hand side the previous solutions of the BDF scheme, the source term and the Dirichlet values.
