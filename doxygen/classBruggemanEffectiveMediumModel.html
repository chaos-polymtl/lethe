Detailed Description

Two-phase Bruggeman effective medium approximation for spherical inclusions. With the void fraction \(\epsilon\) (volume fraction of phase 1), the mixture property \(\alpha_\mathrm{eff}\) is the root of \(\epsilon\frac{\alpha_1 - \alpha_\mathrm{eff}}{\alpha_1 + 2\alpha_\mathrm{eff}} + (1-\epsilon)\frac{\alpha_2 - \alpha_\mathrm{eff}}{\alpha_2 + 2\alpha_\mathrm{eff}} = 0\), a quadratic equation. The root with a positive real part and a non-negative imaginary part is kept.
