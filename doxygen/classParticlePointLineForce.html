Detailed Description

template<int dim, typename PropertiesIndex>
class ParticlePointLineForce< dim, PropertiesIndex >

Calculate the non-linear particle-point and particle-line contact force using the information obtained from the fine search and physical properties of particles and walls. Since the inputs and the calculation method of particle-point and particle-line contact forces are similar, we only used one function for both tasks.

Template Parameters
dimAn integer that denotes the number of spatial dimensions.
PropertiesIndexIndex of the properties used within the ParticleHandler.

Add the displacement that separates a particle from the boundary point or line it overlaps to the displacement of the particle. The displacement is along the normal from the closest point of the boundary to the particle center and is slightly larger than the normal overlap, as in the particle-wall shift model.

Parameters
[in]particleIterator of the particle in contact.
[in]particle_locationLocation of the particle center in 3D.
[in]contact_pointPoint of the boundary point or line that is the closest to the particle center.
[in,out]displacementDisplacement of each local particle.
