4/**
5 * @file continuum_solid_dynamics.h
6 * @brief Auxiliary physics for the solid phase of the Euler-Euler (two-fluid)
7 * model.
8 *
9 * The solid phase is treated as an interpenetrating continuum. Its unknowns
10 * are the solid velocity (dim components) and the solid volume fraction
11 * (1 component), discretized together in a single FESystem. The fluid phase
12 * is solved by the existing volume-averaged Navier-Stokes (VANS) solver.
13 *
14 * In this first version, the class only provides the infrastructure (degrees
15 * of freedom, solution vectors, output, mesh adaptation, checkpointing and
16 * registration in the MultiphysicsInterface). The governing equations are not
17 * assembled yet, so should_solve_auxiliary_physics() returns false.
18 */
19
