Detailed Description

template<int dim>
struct Parameters::QMOMFunctions< dim >

Functions of space and time prescribing the fields of the QMOM physics. They are used for the initial conditions, the Dirichlet boundary conditions, the analytical solution and the source terms.

The scalars are given as a list of expressions, one per scalar, in the "scalars" subsection. The moments are not given one by one: they are the moments of a particle size distribution, defined in the "distribution" subsection by its family and by a list of expressions whose first entry is the number density, in particles per m^3, followed by the parameters of the family.

The number of scalars is not known when the parameters are declared. The number of components of the scalars function is therefore the number of expressions given, and the QMOM physics verifies that it matches the number of scalars once it is known. The scalars function is ignored if the number of scalars is 0.
