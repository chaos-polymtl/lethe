22/**
23 * @brief Calculates the time-averaged velocities and pressure \f$(\langle u
24 * \rangle, \langle v \rangle, \langle w \rangle, \langle p \rangle)\f$ and the
25 * independent components of the Reynolds stresses tensor \f$(\langle u'u'
26 * \rangle, \langle v'v' \rangle, \langle w'w' \rangle, \langle u'v' \rangle,
27 * \langle v'w' \rangle, \langle w'u' \rangle)\f$.
28 *
29 * The Reynolds stresses are computed at every velocity support point from the
30 * degrees of freedom of the velocity components at that point. They are stored
31 * in vectors with the layout of the solution: the normal stresses in the
32 * velocity slots and the turbulent kinetic energy in the pressure slot of the
33 * normal stress vector, and \f$\langle u'v' \rangle\f$, \f$\langle v'w'
34 * \rangle\f$ and \f$\langle w'u' \rangle\f$ in the u, v and w slots of the
35 * shear stress vector. The turbulent kinetic energy is only stored at the
36 * pressure degrees of freedom that share their support point with a velocity
37 * support point.
38 *
39 * @tparam dim An integer that denotes the dimension of the space in which
40 * the flow is solved.
41 *
42 * @tparam VectorType The vector type used for the solvers (Trilinos (normal or block)
43 * or deal.II vectors).
44 *
45 * @tparam DofsType The type of storage of indices which differs according to vector.
46 *
47 */
48template <int dim, typename VectorType, typename DofsType>

51public:

59 * support point. It must outlive the object.

190 /**
191 * @brief Sanitize the average velocity object after a checkpoint has been read by
192 * resetting all of the locally_owned vectors using the locally_relevant
193 * vectors. This is necessary because only the locally_relevant_vectors are
194 * saved, but the calculation routines expect that the content of the
195 * locally_owned vectors match that of the locally_relevant vectors.
196 *
197 * The total time over which the averaging has been carried out is not stored
198 * in the checkpoint file, but it is entirely determined by the time at which
199 * the averaging started, which is stored, and by the time at which the
200 * simulation is restarted. Restoring it here allows the averaged fields to be
201 * reevaluated from the checkpointed sums without accumulating an additional
202 * contribution, which would otherwise be needed to give the averaged fields a
203 * meaningful value right after a restart.
204 *
205 * @param[in] current_time The time at which the simulation is restarted.
206 */
207 void
208 sanitize_after_restart(const double current_time)
209 {
210 sum_velocity_dt = sum_velocity_dt_with_ghost_cells;
211 sum_reynolds_normal_stress_dt = sum_rns_dt_with_ghost_cells;
212 sum_reynolds_shear_stress_dt = sum_rss_dt_with_ghost_cells;
213
214 total_time_for_average =
215 has_started_averaging ? (current_time - real_initial_time) + dt_0 : 0.0;
216
217 update_average_velocities();
218 }

This allows users to restart a simulation with a different averaging start time.
