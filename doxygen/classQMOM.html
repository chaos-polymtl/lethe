Detailed Description

template<int dim>
class QMOM< dim >

Quadrature method of moments (QMOM), solved as an auxiliary physics coupled to the fluid dynamics solver.

Component layout: component i < n_scalars is the scalar i and component n_scalars + k is the moment M_k of the particle size distribution, k < n_moments. This is the ordering of the components of the QMOMFieldFunction of the initial conditions, boundary conditions, source term and analytical solution.

Template Parameters
dimNumber of spatial dimensions.
