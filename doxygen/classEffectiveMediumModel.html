Detailed Description

Effective medium model for the calculation of a physical property of a mixture of two phases. The mixture and its two phases are defined by a material interaction. The property of each phase is calculated with the physical property models of its material. The mixing is done with complex values to allow for the calculation of complex properties, such as the complex permittivity or permeability. The mixture property depends on the fields on which the models of the phases used by the mixing law depend, and on the fields required by the mixing law itself (e.g., the void fraction).
