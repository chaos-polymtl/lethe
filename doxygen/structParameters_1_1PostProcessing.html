Detailed Description

template<int dim>
struct Parameters::PostProcessing< dim >

Postprocessing - Defines the parameters for the post-processing. In Lethe, post-processing implies the calculation of quantities derived from the principal variables. For example, the integral kinetic energy or the integral enstrophy.

Template Parameters
dimDenotes the number of spatial dimensions.
