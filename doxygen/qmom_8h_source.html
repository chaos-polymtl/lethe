4/**
5 * @file qmom.h
6 * @brief Auxiliary physics for the transport of a particle size distribution
7 * with the quadrature method of moments (QMOM), coupled to the QMOMKit
8 * library.
9 *
10 * The unknowns are user-defined scalars and the moments of the particle
11 * size distribution, called the components of the QMOM physics. All the
12 * components are discretized with the same scalar discontinuous Galerkin
13 * element, on a single DoFHandler, and each component is stored in its own
14 * vector. Since all the components are advected by the same fluid velocity,
15 * the matrix of their transport is the same: it is assembled and
16 * preconditioned once per time step, then solved with one right-hand side per
17 * component.
18 *
19 * Each time step of the fluid dynamics is split (Lie splitting) into:
20 * - the transport of all the components in physical space by the fluid
21 * velocity, with their source terms, solved with an upwind discontinuous
22 * Galerkin discretization and an implicit BDF time integration
23 * (advance_physical_space());
24 * - the evolution of the moments in phase space (nucleation, growth and
25 * coalescence), applied independently at every degree of freedom
26 * (advance_phase_space()).
27 *
28 * The transport is linear in the components, since the fluid velocity is
29 * given. The QMOM physics is therefore driven by the linear solver strategy:
30 * assemble_system_matrix() assembles the transport matrix,
31 * setup_preconditioner() builds its preconditioner, assemble_system_rhs()
32 * assembles the right-hand side of every component and solve_linear_system()
33 * advances the components over the time step.
34 */

100/**
101 * @brief Quadrature method of moments (QMOM), solved as an auxiliary physics
102 * coupled to the fluid dynamics solver.
103 *
104 * Component layout: component i < n_scalars is the scalar i and component
105 * n_scalars + k is the moment M_k of the particle size distribution,
106 * k < n_moments. This is the ordering of the components of the
107 * QMOMFieldFunction of the initial conditions, boundary conditions, source
108 * term and analytical solution.
109 *
110 * @tparam dim Number of spatial dimensions.
