12/**
13 * @brief Implementation of a classical velocity verlet scheme for the
14 * integration of the particle motion. Note that reinitialization of force and
15 * torque is also integrated into integration class.
16 *
17 * Velocity Verlet is a second-order integration scheme. It is implemented in
18 * its staggered (leapfrog) form: the velocities are stored at the half steps
19 * and the positions at the integer steps. Consequently, carrying out n time
20 * steps requires n + 1 integration operations.
21 *
22 * Calculation procedure:
23 *
24 * Opening half step (integrate_start), carried
25 * out once at the first time step
26 * v(1/2) = v(0) + 1/2 * a(0) * dt
27 * x(1) = x(0) + v(1/2) * dt
28 *
29 * Regular steps (integrate), carried out at every subsequent time step
30 * a(n) = F(n) / m
31 * v(n+1/2) = v(n-1/2) + a(n) * dt
32 * x(n+1) = x(n) + v(n+1/2) * dt
33 *
34 * Closing half step (integrate_end), carried out once the final
35 * position is reached to synchronize the velocities with the positions
36 * a(n) = F(n) / m
37 * v(n) = v(n-1/2) + 1/2 * a(n) * dt
38 *
39 * @tparam dim An integer that denotes the number of spatial dimensions.
40 * @tparam PropertiesIndex Index of the properties used within the ParticleHandler.
41 *
42 */
43template <int dim, typename PropertiesIndex>
44class VelocityVerletIntegrator : public Integrator<dim, PropertiesIndex>
45{
46public:
47 VelocityVerletIntegrator()
48 {}

50 /**
51 * @brief Integrate the opening half step of the Velocity Verlet scheme, which

55 * consumes a full time step of the simulation, only the velocity is lagging
56 * by dt/2.
57 *
58 * @param particle_handler The particle handler whose particle motion we wish
59 * to integrate
60 * @param body_force A constant volumetric body force applied to all particles
61 * @param time_step The value of the time step used for the integration
62 * @param torque Torque acting on particles. It is reset to zero at the end of
63 * the integration
64 * @param force Force acting on particles. It is reset to zero at the end of
65 * the integration
66 * @param MOI A container of moment of inertia of particles

129 /**
130 * @brief Carry out a regular step of the Velocity Verlet scheme, that is, a full
131 * velocity step followed by a full position step.
132 *
133 * This function is called at every time step but the first one, which is
134 * handled by integrate_start(). It assumes that the velocities are already
135 * staggered, that is, that they are known at t(n-1/2) whereas the positions
136 * are known at t(n), and it preserves that staggering:
137 *
138 * a(n) = g + F(n) / m
139 * v(n+1/2) = v(n-1/2) + a(n) * dt
140 * x(n+1) = x(n) + v(n+1/2) * dt
141 *
142 * The velocity is advanced before the position and the updated velocity is
143 * the one used to advance the position, which is what makes the scheme
144 * second order and symplectic. Note that the acceleration is evaluated from
145 * the force accumulated by the contact force objects since the previous step,
146 * hence the force and the torque containers are reset to zero once they have
147 * been consumed. The angular velocity is advanced in the same manner from the
148 * torque and the moment of inertia.
149 *
150 * All three components of the velocity and of the angular velocity are
151 * advanced, even in 2D, since the third component of the angular velocity is
152 * the one that carries the rotation in 2D. Only the first @p dim components
153 * of the position are advanced.
154 *
155 * @param particle_handler The particle handler whose particle motion we wish
156 * to integrate
157 * @param body_force A constant volumetric body force applied to all particles
158 * @param time_step The value of the time step used for the integration
159 * @param torque Torque acting on particles. It is reset to zero at the end of
160 * the integration
161 * @param force Force acting on particles. It is reset to zero at the end of
162 * the integration
163 * @param MOI A container of moment of inertia of particles
164 */
165 virtual void
166 integrate(Particles::ParticleHandler<dim> &particle_handler,
167 const Tensor<1, 3> &body_force,
168 const double time_step,
169 std::vector<Tensor<1, 3>> &torque,
170 std::vector<Tensor<1, 3>> &force,
171 const std::vector<double> &MOI) override;

173 /**
174 * @brief Carry out a regular step of the Velocity Verlet scheme when the adaptive
175 * sparse contacts are enabled.
176 *
177 * The integration is identical to the one described above, but it is only
178 * carried out for the particles that belong to mobile cells. The particles of
179 * the active and inactive cells only have their force and torque reset, so
180 * that they remain at rest. The particles of the advected cells are displaced
181 * with the average velocity and acceleration of their cell, which is handled
182 * by integrate_with_advected_particles(). When the sparse contacts are
183 * disabled, or on the iteration at which the mobility status is reset, this
184 * function simply falls back to the regular integration.
185 *
186 * @param particle_handler The particle handler whose particle motion we wish
187 * to integrate
188 * @param body_force A constant volumetric body force applied to all particles
189 * @param time_step The value of the time step used for the integration
190 * @param torque Torque acting on particles. It is reset to zero at the end of
191 * the integration
192 * @param force Force acting on particles. It is reset to zero at the end of
193 * the integration
194 * @param MOI A container of moment of inertia of particles
195 * @param triangulation The triangulation of the background mesh
196 * @param sparse_contacts_object The object that stores the mobility status of
197 * the cells
198 */
