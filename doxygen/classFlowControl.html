Detailed Description

template<int dim>
class FlowControl< dim >

Dynamic flow controller that drives the average velocity of the flow towards a target value.

At each time step, the controller calculates a force coefficient beta, applied as a source term in the momentum equation along the flow direction, from the average velocity of the previous time steps. The main controller is based on Wang, 2009, "A non-body conformal grid method for simulations of laminar and turbulent flows with a compressible large eddy simulation solver".

Calculate the beta force coefficient applied during the current time step.

The coefficient adjusts the flow rate towards the target average velocity. Once the target is reached, the controller calculates the beta force that keeps the flow rate constant. The new coefficient is stored and can be obtained with get_beta().

Get the beta force of the current time step scaled for the particles.

The particles are subjected to the same pressure gradient as the fluid, which gives beta_p * rho_p = beta_f * rho_f, in units of a pressure gradient M/(T²L²).

Parameters
[in]fluid_densityDensity of the fluid.
[in]particle_densityDensity of the particles.
Returns
Beta force for the particles. It is a tensor of dimension 3 since it is added to the gravity of the DEM parameters, which is always a tensor of dimension 3.
