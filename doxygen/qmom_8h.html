Detailed Description

Auxiliary physics for the transport of a particle size distribution with the quadrature method of moments (QMOM), coupled to the QMOMKit library.

The unknowns are user-defined scalars and the moments of the particle size distribution, called the components of the QMOM physics. All the components are discretized with the same scalar discontinuous Galerkin element, on a single DoFHandler, and each component is stored in its own vector. Since all the components are advected by the same fluid velocity, the matrix of their transport is the same: it is assembled and preconditioned once per time step, then solved with one right-hand side per component.

Each time step of the fluid dynamics is split (Lie splitting) into:

  • the transport of all the components in physical space by the fluid velocity, with their source terms, solved with an upwind discontinuous Galerkin discretization and an implicit BDF time integration (advance_physical_space());
  • the evolution of the moments in phase space (nucleation, growth and coalescence), applied independently at every degree of freedom (advance_phase_space()).

The transport is linear in the components, since the fluid velocity is given. The QMOM physics is therefore driven by the linear solver strategy: assemble_system_matrix() assembles the transport matrix, setup_preconditioner() builds its preconditioner, assemble_system_rhs() assembles the right-hand side of every component and solve_linear_system() advances the components over the time step.
