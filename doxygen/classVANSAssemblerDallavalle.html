Detailed Description

template<int dim, typename PropertiesIndex>
class VANSAssemblerDallavalle< dim, PropertiesIndex >

Assembler for the drag force using the Dallavalle model.

The drag coefficient is computed as:

\[C_d = \left(0.63 + \frac{4.8}{\sqrt{\text{Re}_p}}\right)^2 \]

and the momentum exchange coefficient for each particle is:

\[\beta = \frac{1}{2} C_d \frac{\pi d_p^2}{4} \|\mathbf{u}_r\| \]

where \( \text{Re}_p \) is the particle Reynolds number, \( d_p \) is the particle diameter, and \( \mathbf{u}_r \) is the relative velocity between the fluid and the particle.
